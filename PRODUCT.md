## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (pinned by the owner), static output, deployed to GitHub Pages (`GustavoBrito446/britol-lp`). Astro 5 because the owner's machine runs Node 20.

## Users

Car owners in São Paulo, mostly men, who just bought (or treat as their pride) a premium or near-premium car: BMW, Mercedes and similar. They arrive mainly from Instagram on a phone, often already curious about PPF or window film, and decide over WhatsApp. They want to feel they are making a smart move that leaves them on top: protecting the asset they care about most, paying less in the long run than they would on bodywork, glass or resale loss.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Britol Protection, a one-person premium vehicle-protection studio run by Gustavo Brito. It sells three service lines and turns visits into a WhatsApp conversation for a quote:

1. **PPF (paint protection film)** in four cumulative packages:
   - **Kit Básico**: door edges (quinas de porta), door-handle cups (conchas de maçaneta), door sills (soleiras).
   - **Kit Start**: everything in Básico + front bumper.
   - **Advanced**: full front (hood, fenders, front bumper) + everything in Básico.
   - **PPF Full**: the whole car.
2. **Anti-vandalism window film**, grades PS4 to PS12 (thicker grade = more resistance).
3. **Solar control window film**: "filme profissional" and "nano carbon".

Success = WhatsApp conversations started for a specific service.

## Positioning

Small, owner-operated studio: the person who answers the WhatsApp is the person who installs the film. Studio work done under a hexagonal LED ceiling, with obsessive finish on high-end cars. "The money doesn't leave your pocket, it stays in the car."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Warranty: 10 years, written.** Confirmed by the owner. Scope (PPF only, or every service) is OPEN; until confirmed, attach it to PPF.
- **Payment: up to 12x on credit card, subject to card-machine interest.** The interest disclaimer must be visible.
- **Pickup and delivery: only for PPF Full.** A transport platform picks the car up at the client's home, takes it to the studio and brings it back.
- Do not promise "bulletproof", "anti-theft guaranteed" or legal outcomes. Window-film darkness must respect Brazilian traffic rules (Contran); state this, don't dodge it.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Britol Protection**. Monogram "BP" in gold on black (Instagram avatar). The black + gold identity is binding.
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese, direct, confident, never cheesy. No emoji in the page.

## Product Principles

1. The client always leaves on top: every section shows what they *gain*, not what they spend.
2. Proof over adjectives: real cars, real finish, real conditions (warranty, 12x, pickup).
3. One honest path to action: WhatsApp, with the service already named.
4. Never invent a claim; when a fact is missing, leave it out.
